How the Chronicle Works
✦
{{ icons.chronicle }}
The Chronicle
Every event of the campaign, in in-world order. Search, filter by character, quest, session, type, place or period, and open any entry to read it in full.
{{ icons.quests }}
Sessions, Quests, Characters & Places
Each has its own section, and everything is linked: from an event jump to its session, quests, people and places — and back again.
{{ icons.map }}
The Map
Loads only when you open it. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom; pins are captioned with their session. Use “Map” at the top of an entry, and “Read entry” from a pin.
{{ icons.search }}
At the table
On a phone, Chronicle, Sessions and Quests sit in the bottom bar; Characters, Places, the Map, light/dark mode and this guide are under More. Your filters and place in the list are kept when you go back. On a keyboard, press / to search.
